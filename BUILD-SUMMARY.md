# DealershipCore Build Summary

The website contains 26 generated pages covering the platform, seven modules, dealership segments, the domain ecosystem, fair competitor comparisons, pricing, company pages, conversion forms and authority guides.

The homepage uses an outcome-first dealership story: operational pain, connected mechanism, product modules, ecosystem advantage, owner outcomes, free systems audit, fair VMG positioning, objection handling and a final founding-dealer call to action.

Product visuals use clearly labelled illustrative data. VMG comparison content separates public VMG capabilities, DealershipCore launch scope and roadmap areas.

The Cloudflare Worker validates enquiries, rejects invalid submissions, supports D1 and webhook persistence, and never reports success when no storage destination is configured.
